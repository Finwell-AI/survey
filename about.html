About Finwell AI

Built for Australians who are tired of guessing at tax time.

Finwell AI turns every purchase into an item-level, tax-ready record — so PAYG employees and sole traders stop leaving money on the table and stop dreading the end of the financial year.

Why we're building this

Bank apps show you "Coles, $87." They don't show you the milk, the bread, the tools, or the work expense buried inside that $87 — the detail that actually decides what you can claim. We're building the bookkeeper that lives in your pocket: AI receipt capture that reads the line items, categorises them against ATO rules, and keeps a live tax ledger so nothing gets lost between July and the next July.
