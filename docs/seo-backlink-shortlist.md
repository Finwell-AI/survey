Vetted list of places to submit Finwell AI to build early domain authority and
topical relevance. Researched 2026-06-03. **Submit manually** (no automation),
one unique description per high-value site, and keep details **consistent**
everywhere (see NAP note below).

> **Context:** Finwell AI is a pre-launch, national, digital-only product with
> no storefront. So we skip location/Maps-only directories and favour
> **startup**, **SaaS/review**, and **sole-trader** directories that accept a
> URL + brand + email without a physical shopfront. Classic local-citation
> sites (Google Business Profile, Apple Maps, Yelp local) are **deprioritised**
> until/unless there's a public business address — see the email reply for why.

## Deprioritised (revisit only with a public address / at launch)
- **Google Business Profile, Apple Maps, Bing Places** — require a verifiable
  physical location / service area; not a fit for a pre-launch national digital
  product. Reconsider if a registered business address is published.
- **Yelp (local)** — local-intent; low value without a physical premise.
- **Yext / BrightLocal (paid citation services)** — only worth it once there's a
  physical-location strategy; the manual Tier 1–3 set covers a digital product.
